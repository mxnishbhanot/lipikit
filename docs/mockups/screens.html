PAY-4127 · Retry job backs off forever — Jira

retry backoff never resets after a 429 so the job sleeps for hours, needs a cap and a reset on success

Jira Comment claude-sonnet-5

Root cause

The backoff multiplier is applied on every attempt but never reset once a request succeeds, so a single 429 leaves the worker sleeping for hours.

Proposed fix

  • Cap the delay at 60s.
  • Reset the multiplier to 1 on the first successful call.
  • Log the delay so the next incident is visible in the dashboard.

Happy to split the cap and the reset into two commits if that reviews easier.
